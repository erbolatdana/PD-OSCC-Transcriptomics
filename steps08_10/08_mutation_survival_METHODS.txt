PURPOSE AND REPRODUCIBILITY STATUS

This stage was performed using web-based tools; no local R computation was
used for the original analyses. The instructions below document the settings
and results recovered from the study records. They support review of the
reported results, but do not by themselves guarantee exact reproduction.
Some original query settings, data exports and access dates remain unverified.
Do not infer unrecorded parameters or tune a new query to match the results.

Five selected genes:
    CXCL1, CXCL6, CXCR4, RGS1, CHST2

================================================================================
PART A - SOMATIC ALTERATIONS AND ONCOPRINT (cBioPortal)
================================================================================

Platform: https://www.cbioportal.org
Study: Head and Neck Squamous Cell Carcinoma (TCGA, PanCancer Atlas)
Study ID: hnsc_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018
Study URL: https://www.cbioportal.org/study?id=hnsc_tcga_pan_can_atlas_2018
Reported cohort: 496 samples. The sample-to-patient mapping and the exact
eligibility criteria and denominators for each result require verification.

Documented workflow:
    1. Select the study by the study ID above.
    2. Query CXCL1, CXCL6, CXCR4, RGS1 and CHST2.
    3. View the OncoPrint and the alteration-status survival comparison.

Original OncoPrint labels in the saved image:
    Gene       Alteration frequency
    CXCL1      1.6%
    CXCL6      1.0%
    CXCR4      0.6%
    RGS1       1.4%
    CHST2      7.0%

The manuscript reports rounded frequencies of 2%, 1%, <1%, 1% and 7%,
respectively. These are gene-level alteration frequencies, not mutation-only
frequencies. The saved OncoPrint legend includes mutations, copy-number
alterations and structural variants. The original selected molecular profiles,
filters, sample set and query options must be verified from archived settings
or exports; the image alone does not establish every query parameter.

The sum of the displayed percentages is approximately 11.6%. Adding
gene-level frequencies counts an individual more than once if they have
alterations in multiple genes. Consequently, the sum is not an estimate of
the proportion of unique altered patients. Because displayed frequencies are
rounded and denominators and sample/patient mapping require verification,
11.6% should not be presented as an exact bound from the saved image alone.
The actual unique altered and unaltered proportions should be reported from
the relevant patient-level export once the denominator is verified.

================================================================================
PART B - OVERALL SURVIVAL BY ALTERATION STATUS (cBioPortal)
================================================================================

The archived analysis compares overall survival for cases classified as
altered versus unaltered under the five-gene query. Verify the exact alteration
profiles, group definitions, patient eligibility and survival endpoint against
the original query or exported data before describing them as fully recovered.

The saved survival figure shows:
    Altered group:   50 cases at risk at time zero
    Unaltered group: 446 cases at risk at time zero
    Log-rank p:      0.269

The study record and manuscript additionally report:
    Altered:   27 events; median overall survival 35.54 months
    Unaltered: 184 events; median overall survival 57.93 months

The archived methods file reported 95% median-survival intervals of
26.50 to NA months and 48.20 to 84.49 months, respectively. These medians,
event counts and intervals are transcribed from the study record; they are not
independently verified by the saved survival image. The unavailable upper
interval bound must not be presented as a known value.

In this plotted survival comparison, 446 / 496 is approximately 89.9%.
This describes the plotted groups. Do not generalize it to a different sample
set or to all study patients until the underlying case mapping and eligibility
have been checked.

Interpretation: the reported log-rank test did not detect a statistically
significant difference. This result does not establish equivalence or the
absence of an association or prognostic effect.

The different group totals are reported as displayed; the reason for the
differences is not established by the saved figures. The figures support the
reported p-values and hazard ratios, but do not document every original query
option or provide individual patient-level expression and survival records.
Record the original access date, dataset/version, expression source, cutoff
settings, patient exclusions and any other query settings if evidence is found.

All five reported log-rank p-values exceed 0.05. Non-significance does not
establish that a prognostic association is absent or clinically negligible.

================================================================================
RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN THE ANALYSES AND AUDIT MATERIALS
================================================================================

Part B groups cases by genomic alteration status. Part C groups cases by
expression level for each gene. These answer different questions, but both
use TCGA-HNSC data and may include overlapping patients. They must not be
described as independent cohorts without verifying the patient sets.
Neither analysis establishes a causal mechanism or the absence of a
prognostic role.

Recovered study evidence:
    - Saved OncoPrint image with the five gene-level percentages.
    - Saved cBioPortal overall-survival image with the plotted group sizes
      and log-rank p-value.
    - Five saved individual GEPIA2 survival plots.
    - Manuscript Methods and Results and the earlier Step 08 methods record.

For an independent audit, preserve the query links or settings, access dates,
study and profile identifiers, sample/patient selection and relevant exported
alteration, clinical and survival tables when available. Archive public-source
data in line with the applicable data-access terms. Exact historical results
may depend on the database version and settings. A new query should be labelled
with its access date and should not be presented as the archived analysis.

The Step 08 audit does not rerun or modify the original analysis.
